Sticky Paws Privacy Information
Version: sticky-diagnostics-1
Updated: November 15, 2026
Language: English. Bundled local copy for this development version.

Your choices

You can play and read this information offline without an account or telemetry
permission. Options > Telemetry Data explains fields and effective saved
choices. Automatic diagnostic recording and sending start Off. Configure data
alone, or categories alone, do not start capture. Device, broad game-context
and performance choices are separate. No gameplay/editor usage analytics is
part of this diagnostic implementation.

Diagnostic reports

Remote diagnostic sending is unavailable: this development version has no
verified receiver configured. It never uses the former raw-INI upload route.
A local record or completed network operation is not proof of server storage.

If you deliberately enable local crash recording, the minimized record uses
a schema, crash kind, random per-report ID, authored build label, Windows
platform, notice version and safe error code. There is no permanent player,
device, client or session identifier. Included Data explains optional fields.
Unavailable or declined values stay absent. Automatic records exclude OS,
creator and device names, platform IDs, local/project paths, secrets, custom
content, arbitrary exception text and unrestricted global/INI dumps.
Optional code frames contain only mapped authored resources/events and valid lines.
Allowed selected information can still describe an error and coarse context;
we do not promise that every diagnostic value is anonymous or non-personal.

Manual review and export

After an error, or choosing a retained safe record, you may create a separate
manual draft. Carefully review exact contents and any summary you type. Do not
include passwords, tokens, personal details or private content. Optional data
and summary can be removed. The draft is memory-only, discarded on Cancel,
close or restart. Review expires after 30 minutes or earlier on relevant
changes. It never enables future automatic collection.

Export reviewed copy saves an independent local copy and sends nothing.
Sharing it later follows the chosen recipient's rules and visibility. Public
bug attachments may be visible to others. Exported/synced copies are outside
managed cleanup. A new manual report gets a fresh ID and could duplicate an
earlier submission whose receipt was uncertain.

Local storage and deletion

Optional local originals: at most 14 days, 20 records and 5 MiB total.
Local status/receipts: at most 30 days, 50 entries and 64 KiB total.
Cleanup runs on startup/active cleanup while the game runs, not while closed.
Failed cleanup is shown honestly. Viewing/restart never renews original age.
View My Data works offline. Off stops new automatic records, sampling,
enrollment, uploads and retries; existing files remain until expiry/deletion.

Discard pending and Delete local records have different scopes. Legacy raw
debug/crash INIs remain separate, may include information excluded from new
reports, and are never automatically uploaded or migrated into the new queue.
Their deliberate review/export/deletion controls explain what is affected.

Local deletion does not remove server/hosting copies. Dispatched bytes may
have arrived after timeout/cancellation. A verified remote removal process is
not available through this implementation. Current access/retention for
historical remote reports is unverified; no new remote-expiry promise is made.

Online creations and platforms

Deliberate online sharing/download features process uploaded levels,
characters, creator attribution and metadata for that feature. Shared content
may be available to other players. Creator name credits your uploads; it is
separate from diagnostics. Avoid personal/private information in shared content.
These features use server requests, authentication and request/security
metadata independently of diagnostic JSON. Servers can process source IP and
request time. The local diagnostic viewer does not contain every server,
hosting or platform log.

Steam, Nintendo and the operating system process information separately under
their own policies. Off does not disable platform reporting or online actions
you request. Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
Nintendo: https://www.nintendo.com/privacy-policy/

Contact and the website copy

For privacy questions, contact contact@jonnil.games. Avoid attaching unrestricted
logs or sensitive content to public requests. Reading this local copy does not
contact that address or open a browser. Read on website is a separate deliberate
action; the web copy can change independently. A web update never silently
expands a saved diagnostic choice or the scope described here.
